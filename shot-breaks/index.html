Round 00Overview

Why ashotbreaks.

Every padel shot is four steps. When one step fails, the shot fails. Scroll to watch one shot happen.

Round 01See

You hitwhat yousee.

Read the ball early: how fast, how high, and where it will bounce.

Read more
  • DoWatch the other player's racket face as they hit. It tells you the direction before the ball does.
  • DoCall the bounce in your head: short, deep, or off the glass.
  • It broke here ifThe ball surprised you, or you moved late or the wrong way.

Round 02Decide

One ball,one plan.

Pick the shot before the ball arrives. Then commit to it.

Read more
  • DoChoose before the bounce, not during the swing.
  • DoKeep a default. If you are unsure, lob.
  • It broke here ifYou changed your mind mid-swing, or went for the hard shot when the easy one was open.

Round 03Set

Feetfirst.

Get behind the ball with the racket ready, and be balanced before you hit.

Read more
  • DoTake small steps to adjust, and prepare the racket while you move.
  • DoLeave space. The ball should be in front of you, an arm's length away.
  • It broke here ifThe ball was too close or too far, or you were still moving at contact.

Round 04Hit

Let itgo.

Contact in front, a smooth swing, and a full finish.

Read more
  • DoKeep the grip loose and finish the swing toward your target.
  • KnowIf the first three steps were right, this is the easy part. Many "bad technique" misses are a late See or a poor Set.
  • It broke here ifYou were in the right place with the right plan and still rushed or tightened the swing.

Round 05Body + Mind

Thefuel.

All four steps run on your body and your mind. When the fuel is low, good technique still breaks.

Read more
  • BodyLegs, breath, water, sleep. Tired legs make every Set late.
  • MindFocus and calm. A head stuck on the last point makes every See late.
  • It broke here ifThe same shot worked in the first set and stopped working in the third.

Road to Pro 2027

01 · A letterVitto Alexander

I played tennis for almost twenty years before I picked up a padel racket, with three years of pickleball in Seattle and a golf handicap of 13 along the way.

I first tried padel in August 2025 and barely touched it for six months. On Valentine's Day 2026 I fell in love with the game. Since then I have played 23 tournaments with 19 different partners, won a Silver title with my dad, and reached a Gold final in October. I have lost plenty of matches too, and those taught me the most.

I'd rather keep learning than get everything right. That is how I train and how I'd work with a partner: show up, be honest about where I am, and get a little better every week.

I have always been relationship first, so read this as an introduction. 2027 is my road to pro. If you are a brand, a community or a club and something here fits, I'd like to meet, hit some balls and see where it goes.
